# Local SEO Checklist for Mr. Robert's Fries

## 1. Google Business Profile (HIGHEST PRIORITY)

Claim at: https://www.google.com/business

Setup:
- Business name: "Mr. Robert's Fries — East Legon"
  (include the location, but do NOT keyword stuff)
- Category: Primary = "Fast Food Restaurant"
           Secondary = "Chicken Restaurant", "Delivery Service"
- NAP (Name/Address/Phone) — SAME AS YOUR WEBSITE:
  Name: Mr. Robert's Fries
  Address: Boundary Road, East Legon, Accra, Greater Accra, Ghana
  Phone: +233 59 923 3488
- Website: https://mrfries.com
- Hours: Mon-Sun 11:00-23:00
- Service area: East Legon, UG Legon, UPSA, Madina, Adenta, Adjiringanor
- Attributes: Delivery, Takeaway, Dine-in, Wheelchair accessible, etc.
- Photos: 20+ high-quality — menu items, storefront, kitchen
- Products: Add each menu item with photo + price

## 2. Google Business Profile — Kumasi branch

Create a SECOND GBP listing for the KNUST location:
- Name: "Mr. Robert's Fries — KNUST Bomso"
- Address: Bomso Road, near KNUST, Kumasi, Ashanti, Ghana
- Service area: Bomso, Ayeduase, KNUST Campus, Adum, Kejetia

## 3. Local Citations (Kill two birds at once)

Register at these Ghanaian directories with IDENTICAL NAP info:

- [ ] yellowpages.com.gh

- [ ] businesslist.com.gh
- [ ] ghanaweb.com (Business section)
- [ ] ghanatradedirectory.com

Consistency is critical — same Name, same Address, same Phone on every single listing.

## 4. Reviews (Biggest single local SEO lever)

- After delivery: SMS with a direct Google Review link
  (Get it from your GBP → Share → Review link)
- Target: 20+ reviews in the first month
- Reply to EVERY review within 24 hours
- Ask customers to upload photos with their review (kills two signals at once)

## 6. Local link building

- Sponsor a UG Legon student event → get a link on the university page
- Partner with 2-3 Ghanaian food bloggers → guest post or review
- List on Hubtel and Glovo (even if just for the backlink)
- Get mentioned in local news (e.g., "Best new eats in Accra — 2026")
