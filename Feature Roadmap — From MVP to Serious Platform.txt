# Feature Roadmap — From MVP to Serious Platform

You've built a working ordering system. Here's what separates a class project from a real product. I've organized by **impact**, **effort**, and **when to add it**. Don't build all of these — pick from each tier based on what your goal is (impress users, learn tech, or run a real business).

---

## 🥇 Tier 1 — Must-haves for a real business (build these first)

### 1. Real-time order tracking
**What:** Customer sees live status without refreshing — "Preparing" → "Out for delivery" → "Delivered".
**How:** WebSockets via FastAPI (`python-socketio` or native WebSocket). Or simpler: **poll every 10s** with `setInterval` in the tracking page.
**Why it matters:** The #1 customer complaint in food delivery is "where's my order?". Fix it and you win repeat business.
**Effort:** Medium (polling) / High (WebSockets).

### 2. SMS + WhatsApp notifications
**What:** Send an SMS when order is paid, when it's out for delivery, and when delivered.
**How:** **Arkesel** (Ghana-friendly SMS) or **Twilio**. For WhatsApp: **Meta Cloud API** (free tier) or **360dialog**.
**Why:** Ghanaian customers live on WhatsApp. A "Your order is on the way 🛵" message is worth 10 emails.
**Effort:** Low-Medium.

### 3. Admin CRUD — full menu & zone management
**What:** Add/edit/delete menu items, builder options, delivery zones, without ever touching the code.
**How:** Forms on your admin dashboard calling your existing API endpoints.
**Why:** Otherwise every new item requires a developer. That's a business killer.
**Effort:** Medium.

### 4. Email receipts (transactional email)
**What:** Automatic PDF/inline receipt to customer after successful payment.
**How:** **Resend** or **Postmark**. Send from a background task after webhook `charge.success`.
**Why:** Professionalism + customers have proof.
**Effort:** Low.

### 5. Order state machine with guards
**What:** Prevent invalid transitions — e.g. `delivered` can't go back to `pending`, only staff can mark `out_for_delivery`.
**How:** Encode allowed transitions in `app/models/order.py` and validate in `crud/order.py`.
**Why:** Prevents accidental destructive changes as your team grows.
**Effort:** Low.

### 6. Rate limiting & abuse protection
**What:** Prevent a bot from hammering `/api/v1/orders` or `/api/v1/auth/login`.
**How:** `slowapi` middleware. 5 req/min per IP on orders, 3 req/min on login.
**Why:** Prevents DDoS, brute-force, and accidental double-submits.
**Effort:** Low.

### 7. Duplicate order prevention (idempotency keys)
**What:** If a customer's network blips and the request retries, don't create 2 orders.
**How:** Client sends `X-Idempotency-Key: <uuid>`, server stores it for 10 min and returns the same order.
**Why:** Real problem on unreliable networks.
**Effort:** Low.

### 8. Refunds & cancellations
**What:** Customer requests cancel before `preparing`; refund via Paystack `/refund`.
**How:** `POST /api/v1/orders/{id}/cancel` and `POST /api/v1/payments/{ref}/refund`.
**Why:** Trust. Paystack has a refund API — 20 lines of code.
**Effort:** Low.

## 🥈 Tier 2 — Differentiators (build when you have paying customers)

### 9. Live kitchen display system (KDS)
**What:** A separate screen for the kitchen showing incoming orders in real time, with timers.
**How:** React page polling `/api/v1/orders?status=paid` every 5s, big buttons for "Mark preparing" → "Ready".
**Why:** Your staff will love it. It's how real restaurants operate.
**Effort:** Medium.

### 10. Delivery rider app / portal
**What:** Riders see assigned orders, tap "Picked up" / "Delivered", and customers see "Rider is 5 min away".
**How:** Simple mobile-friendly page `/rider` with JWT login. Rider role in `UserRole`.
**Why:** Cuts the "where is my food?" calls to zero.
**Effort:** Medium-High.

### 11. Loyalty program
**What:** Points system — 1 GH₵ spent = 1 point; 500 points = free loaded bowl.
**How:** Add `loyalty_points` to `User`, an anonymous `customer_id` cookie, and a `POST /api/v1/loyalty/redeem`.
**Why:** Increases repeat orders by 20–40% in food businesses.
**Effort:** Medium.

### 12. Promo codes / discounts
**What:** `UGLEGON10` gives 10% off; `FREESHIP` waives delivery fee.
**How:** `PromoCode` model with `code`, `discount_type` (% or fixed), `max_uses`, `expires_at`, `min_order`.
**Why:** Marketing needs this. Also great for IG campaigns.
**Effort:** Medium.

### 13. Delivery fee zones with distance calculation
**What:** Compute delivery fee dynamically based on distance from kitchen to address.
**How:** **Google Maps Distance Matrix API** or **Mapbox**. Pass coordinates at checkout, server computes.
**Why:** Fair pricing, no more fixed tiers.
**Effort:** Medium.

### 14. Push notifications (web)
**What:** "Your order is ready!" even when the tab is closed.
**How:** Web Push API + `pywebpush`. Store subscriptions in Mongo.
**Why:** Increases repeat orders, especially when the customer might forget.
**Effort:** Medium.

### 15. Reviews & ratings
**What:** After delivery, customer rates the meal 1–5 stars + comment.
**How:** `Review` model linked to `Order` + `MenuItem`. Show average on menu cards.
**Why:** Social proof drives sales. Also feeds back on which items to keep.
**Effort:** Low-Medium.

### 16. Favorites & reorder
**What:** "Order again" button on past orders, plus a heart icon on menu items.
**How:** Store `favorites: List[MenuItem]` per customer; one-click reorder creates a new order with the same items.
**Why:** Reduces friction. Repeat customers are your bread and butter.
**Effort:** Low.

### 17. Scheduling / pre-orders
**What:** "Deliver at 7:00 PM" or "Order for lunch tomorrow".
**How:** Add `scheduled_for: datetime` to Order. Cron job (APScheduler or Celery) triggers payment or moves to `preparing`.
**Why:** Corporate lunch orders. Big ticket size.
**Effort:** Medium-High.

### 18. Multi-branch support
**What:** Two kitchens (Accra + Kumasi) with separate menus, zones, staff, and analytics.
**How:** Add `branch_id` to relevant models. Staff see only their branch. Analytics filter by branch.
**Why:** Required when you scale. Do it before you need it, not after.
**Effort:** High.

## 🥉 Tier 3 — Nice-to-have / cool factor

### 19. AI-powered recommendations
**What:** "Because you ordered gizzard, try the peppered sausage."
**How:** Simple collaborative filtering, or OpenAI/Gemini API with prompt "Given this order history, suggest 3 items from this menu."
**Why:** Increases average order value. Also fun demo.
**Effort:** Medium.

### 20. WhatsApp bot ordering
**What:** Customer messages "Hi" on WhatsApp, bot shows menu, takes order, sends Paystack link.
**How:** Meta Cloud API webhook → FastAPI. Twilio WhatsApp as a shortcut.
**Why:** Ghana-friendly. No app install needed. Huge market advantage here.
**Effort:** High.

### 21. Voice ordering (AI)
**What:** Customer calls a number, AI takes the order.
**How:** Twilio Voice + Deepgram (STT) + OpenAI + ElevenLabs (TTS).
**Why:** Very cool demo. Probably premature for real use.
**Effort:** Very High.

### 22. Photo uploads by customers
**What:** After delivery, customer uploads a photo of their bowl → goes into a "gallery" for social proof.
**How:** You already have the `/api/v1/uploads` endpoint. Just add a public `Review` with `photos: List[str]`.
**Effort:** Low.

### 23. QR code menu / table tent
**What:** QR code on every bag/flyer that opens your menu with a specific promo code pre-applied.
**How:** `qrcode` Python library + query params like `?promo=UG10`.
**Why:** Cheap marketing. Measurable attribution.
**Effort:** Very Low.

### 24. Kitchen automation (printer integration)
**What:** When an order is paid, automatically print a ticket in the kitchen.
**How:** **Epson ePOS SDK** for network printers, or a small Raspberry Pi running `python-escpos`.
**Why:** Eliminates human error in busy kitchens.
**Effort:** Medium-High (hardware).

### 25. Analytics dashboard with charts
**What:** Revenue chart, daily orders, hourly heatmap, top zones.
**How:** **Recharts** or **Chart.js** on the frontend. Your backend already returns the daily series.
**Why:** Visuals speak louder than numbers.
**Effort:** Low.

### 26. A/B testing on the home page
**What:** Show two hero images, measure which drives more conversions.
**How:** Random flag per session, log `POST /api/v1/events`.
**Why:** Optimizes conversion by 5–15% typically.
**Effort:** Medium.

### 27. Referral program
**What:** "Share your code, both you and your friend get 10 GH₵ off."
**How:** `ReferralCode` per user, `Referred_By` on new signups, apply at checkout.
**Why:** Viral growth. Costs nothing until used.
**Effort:** Medium.

## 🔧 Backend / infrastructure improvements

### 28. Background job queue
**What:** Do slow work (emails, SMS, image resizing, Paystack refunds) in the background, not in the request.
**How:** **APScheduler** for simple jobs, or **Celery** + Redis for bigger scale, or **Arq** (async Redis queue) — best fit for FastAPI.
**Why:** Requests return in 50ms instead of 2s. Prevents timeouts.
**Effort:** Medium.

### 29. Structured logging & monitoring
**What:** Every request logged with trace ID, errors forwarded to a service.
**How:** **Loguru** + **Sentry**. Ship logs to **Logtail** or **Papertrail**.
**Why:** When something breaks at 9 PM on Friday, you'll know.
**Effort:** Low.

### 30. Proper test suite
**What:** Full pytest coverage of every endpoint with a test DB.
**How:** Your `tests/` scaffold already exists. Write tests for happy + error paths.
**Why:** Confidence to refactor. Also, most job interviews ask about this.
**Effort:** Medium.

### 31. CI/CD pipeline
**What:** Every push runs tests, builds Docker image, deploys if green.
**How:** **GitHub Actions** → build → push to **Render** / **Railway** / **Fly.io**.
**Why:** Ship confidently, multiple times a day.
**Effort:** Medium.

### 32. Redis caching
**What:** Cache menu, categories, delivery zones for 60s so every page load doesn't hit Mongo.
**How:** `redis` + `fastapi-cache2`.
**Why:** 10x faster menu page under load. Trivial to add.
**Effort:** Low.

### 33. Health checks & readiness probes
**What:** `/health` (already there) + `/ready` that pings Mongo.
**How:** Add a Mongo ping to `/health`.
**Why:** Required for orchestrators (Kubernetes, Render, Fly).
**Effort:** Very Low.

### 34. Image CDN
**What:** Serve menu images from Cloudinary/S3 + Cloudflare instead of local disk.
**How:** Swap `file_storage.py` to upload to Cloudinary, return the CDN URL.
**Why:** Disk fills up. CDN is faster. Local uploads vanish on redeploy.
**Effort:** Low.

### 35. Backup strategy
**What:** Daily MongoDB backup to S3, 30-day retention.
**How:** **MongoDB Atlas** has automatic backups. If self-hosted, use `mongodump` + cron + S3.
**Why:** Losing order data is a company-ending event.
**Effort:** Low.

## 📱 Frontend improvements

### 36. PWA (Progressive Web App)
**What:** Installable on the home screen, works offline, feels like a native app.
**How:** `next-pwa` package. Add manifest + service worker.
**Why:** Ghana customers have limited storage — no app store install needed.
**Effort:** Low.

### 37. Skeleton loaders
**What:** Instead of "Loading menu..." show grey cards pulsing.
**How:** Add skeleton components (literally 5 lines each).
**Why:** Perceived performance. Feels 2x faster.
**Effort:** Very Low.

### 38. Optimistic UI updates
**What:** When customer adds to cart, update UI instantly, sync with server in background.
**How:** React 19's `useOptimistic` hook.
**Why:** Feels instant. Eliminates waiting.
**Effort:** Low.

### 39. Image optimization
**What:** Use Next.js `<Image>` component everywhere.
**How:** Swap `<img>` for `<Image>` — you'll get WebP, lazy loading, and responsive sizes for free.
**Why:** 60% smaller images = 3x faster on 3G.
**Effort:** Very Low.

### 40. Dark / light mode toggle
**What:** Some customers browse in sunlight, some at night.
**How:** CSS variables + `next-themes`.
**Why:** Accessibility. Also, your design is dark — many will want light.
**Effort:** Low.

### 41. Multi-language (Twi, Ga, Ewe)
**What:** Localized UI for non-English speakers.
**How:** `next-intl`. Start with English + Twi.
**Why:** Broadens your addressable market.
**Effort:** Medium.

### 42. Accessibility
**What:** Screen reader support, keyboard navigation, WCAG AA contrast.
**How:** `eslint-plugin-jsx-a11y`, manual testing with VoiceOver/NVDA.
**Why:** Right thing to do. Also, some customers literally can't order without it.
**Effort:** Low-Medium.

## 💰 Business & operations features

### 43. Inventory management
**What:** Track stock of ingredients. Auto-mark items out of stock when low.
**How:** `Ingredient` model + `stock_count`. Deduct on each paid order. Alert when below threshold.
**Why:** Stops selling what you can't make.
**Effort:** Medium-High.

### 44. Cost & profit tracking
**What:** Know the margin on each item. Hide this from staff.
**How:** Add `cost_price` to `MenuItem`. Analytics shows `revenue - cost = profit`.
**Why:** You can't run a business without knowing margins.
**Effort:** Low.

### 45. Staff shifts & payroll
**What:** Clock in/out for staff. Track hours.
**How:** `Shift` model, simple UI on admin.
**Why:** If you hire more than 3 people, you'll want this.
**Effort:** Medium.

### 46. Customer feedback loop
**What:** After delivery, 3-question survey via SMS.
**How:** Postmark/Arkesel template with a link to `/feedback/{orderId}`.
**Why:** Catch bad experiences before they hit Google reviews.
**Effort:** Low.

### 47. Gift cards
**What:** Buy a GH₵ 100 gift card, email the code, redeem at checkout.
**How:** `GiftCard` model, `POST /api/v1/giftcards/purchase`, apply at checkout.
**Why:** Corporate gifts, birthdays. Easy money.
**Effort:** Medium.

### 48. Subscription meal plans
**What:** "5 lunches per week, charged every Monday."
**How:** Paystack **subscriptions** API. `Subscription` model.
**Why:** Predictable revenue. Great for corporate clients.
**Effort:** High.

### 49. Multi-payment methods
**What:** Add cash on delivery, bank transfer, crypto.
**How:** Add `payment_method` enum on Order. Cash goes to `pending` → `paid` when rider collects.
**Why:** Some customers don't have MoMo set up.
**Effort:** Low-Medium.

### 50. Franchise / white-label
**What:** Sell your system to other food businesses.
**How:** Multi-tenancy with `tenant_id` on every model. Separate subdomain per tenant.
**Why:** Whole new revenue stream.
**Effort:** Very High.
